About me

I am a researcher developing statistical methods for health equity and environment–health research. My primary interests include measurement error models, underreporting, and missing data as they arise in administrative and other non-representative data sources.

Previously, I was based in Pakistan and Ethiopia working for the Center of Evaluation and Development, a research consulting firm specialising in quantitative impact evaluations and mixed-methods analyses across social protection, poverty reduction, and education.
